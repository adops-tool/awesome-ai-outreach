A curated list of AI-powered tools, autonomous SDRs, open-source projects, and agents for lead generation, social listening, data enrichment, AI search visibility (GEO), and automated outreach.

The goal of this repository is to document the **Modern Growth Stack**, moving away from static databases and towards intent-based, AI-driven customer acquisition across social, email, website intent, and AI search.

## B2B Intent Data
*Third-party intent signals that identify accounts actively researching a product category, before they contact you.*

![web](image/alg_I.png) [6sense](https://6sense.com) - Predictive account intent platform that identifies companies actively researching a product category.

![web](image/alg_I.png) [Bombora](https://bombora.com) - B2B buyer intent data aggregated from a co-op of industry publisher sites.

![web](image/alg_I.png) [Demandbase](https://demandbase.com) - Account-based platform combining firmographic data with buyer intent signals.

![web](image/alg_I.png) [G2](https://www.g2.com) - Software review marketplace that sells buyer intent data on accounts researching a product category.

![web](image/alg_I.png) [Picked](https://picked.so) - Tracks AI search visibility and generates content optimized to rank and be cited across Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude.

![web](image/alg_I.png) [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/) - Enterprise platform that tracks brand visibility, prompt volumes, and citations across answer engines.

![web](image/alg_I.png) [Scrunch](https://scrunch.com) - Tracks how AI models crawl, cite, and represent a brand, with site optimization for AI agents.

![web](image/alg_I.png) [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) - SEO platform with AI visibility tracking for how brands appear in LLM answers.
